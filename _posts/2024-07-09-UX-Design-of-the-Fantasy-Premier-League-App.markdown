Introduction
A quick look at how the Fantasy Premier League app employe the UX design—usable, equitable, enjoyable, and useful—in principles

<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/Fantasy Football.jpg" alt="Fantasy Football">

Usable 
* The 'Latest', 'PL', 'Fantasy', 'Challenge' buttons are examples of usable design as they indicate what will happen if you interact with them. However, it could be argued that it's not entirely clear what exactly you get when you click on them in terms of the resulting pages.
* 
Equitable 
* In terms of equity, there's potentially a lot of room for improvement. The app is primarily geared towards men's football, news, and information. It might be an idea to include women's football, for example, or even activities and information from non-Premier League teams. However, as this is specifically a Premier League fantasy football app, this may not be a significant issue within its specific value proposition.

Enjoyable 
* The app is very enjoyable. It's visually appealing, the subject matter is interesting, and the fun nature of the product makes it quite enjoyable. Its ability to bring friends together in a competitive environment adds to its appeal.
* Potential ideas for improvement could include some kind of interaction between players, especially when the games are live. This might add a layer of engagement that would enhance the enjoyment of the app.

Useful 
* The app is very useful in the sense that it allows you to play fantasy football, stay up-to-date on fixtures, and access general information on Premier League teams.
